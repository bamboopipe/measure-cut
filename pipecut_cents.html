Open pipe: length to cut for a pitch change

Notes

Pitch is inversely proportional to length, and 1200 cents make one octave (a factor of 2). Raising the pitch by c cents therefore scales the length by 2^(-c/1200), and the cut is L × (1 − 2^(-c/1200)). A negative value lowers the pitch, so the pipe would have to be lengthened.

This page ignores the bore diameter and the end correction, which typically adds a few tenths of a millimetre. The full page, pipecut.html, includes it.
